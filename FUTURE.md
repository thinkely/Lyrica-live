# Lyrica Live — Future Roadmap & Backlog (Post-V1)

This document records ideas, enhancements, and extended integrations deliberately scoped out of **Lyrica Live v1** to maintain a tight, privacy-first, and lightweight architecture.

---

## 1. Cross-Service & Library Integrations
- Universal Spotify / YouTube Music / Apple Music playlist synchronization.
- Cross-service playlist migration and listening history logging (Last.fm / ListenBrainz scrobbling).
- Cross-device playback sync.

## 2. Social & Community Systems
- Community translation and transliteration sharing network.
- Public translation database and user-curated corrections.
- Social profiles, lyric sharing cards, and listening presence.

## 3. Extended AI & Audio Features
- On-device whisper audio recognition for ambient music lyric identification.
- AI-generated dynamic lyric translations and localized romanization.
- Music emotion and real-time audio visualizer overlays.

## 4. Platform Expansions
- Desktop companion (macOS / Windows / Linux status bar tray app).
- Wear OS companion app with offline synced lyrics display.
- Android Auto / Automotive OS customized display.
